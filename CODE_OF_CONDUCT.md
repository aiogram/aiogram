# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
* Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community
* Helping newcomers: a question that looks trivial to you may be someone's
  first step with asyncio and Telegram bots

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of
  any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  phone number, or Telegram account details, without their explicit permission
* Impersonating other community members or maintainers, or creating accounts to
  evade a ban
* Spam, unsolicited advertising, scams, and mass unsolicited messages to
  community members
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Community Spaces

The spaces run by the aiogram project are:

* the GitHub repositories of the [aiogram organization](https://github.com/aiogram),
  including issues, discussions, and pull requests
* the Telegram chat [@aiogram](https://t.me/aiogram) and its language-specific
  sister chats listed in the project README
* the news channel [@aiogram_live](https://t.me/aiogram_live)
* the listings on [aiogram.dev](https://aiogram.dev)

The expectations below apply in those spaces on top of the general standards
above.

### On GitHub

* Keep discussions technical and on topic. Disagree with ideas, not people.
* Before opening an issue, search for existing ones. Fill in the issue and pull
  request templates: they exist to save everyone's time.
* Bug reports, feature requests, and pull requests must be made in good faith.
  Submitting content you have not read or tested yourself, including unreviewed
  machine-generated code or reports, wastes maintainers' time and may be closed
  without discussion.
* Review feedback is about the code, not about you. Maintainers may ask for
  changes, decline a contribution, or close an issue; this is a normal part of
  the process, not a judgement of the author.
* Do not "bump" issues or pull requests, ping maintainers repeatedly, or demand
  timelines. aiogram is developed by volunteers in their free time.

### In Telegram chats

* Follow the language of the chat. Each chat has its own working language; if
  you are not sure where to ask, use the English chat [@aiogram](https://t.me/aiogram).
* Ask questions in public, not in private messages to maintainers or helpers,
  unless invited to do so. Public answers help the next person with the same
  problem.
* Help others the way you would like to be helped: a short pointer to the
  documentation is fine, "read the docs" alone is not.
* Do not post job offers, paid-work requests, or advertising unless the chat
  rules explicitly allow it. Do not ask others to write your bot for you.
* Do not share content that violates the law, the
  [Telegram Terms of Service](https://telegram.org/tos), or the
  [Telegram Bot API Terms of Service](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api-tos).
  This includes bots built for spam, fraud, or abuse of other users.
* Chat administrators moderate their chat and may set additional rules. Their
  decisions are subject to this Code of Conduct and may be appealed as
  described below.

### Security issues

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues and must not be discussed in
public. Report them privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Participation restrictions

This section is specific to aiogram and is a deliberate exception to the pledge
above, made because of russia's war against Ukraine, where aiogram is developed.

Individuals, companies and state entities from russia, belarus and iran are not
welcome in aiogram-run community spaces, may not be listed on aiogram.dev, and
may be removed from those spaces without discussion. The maintainers may extend
this list.

This governs participation in aiogram's own spaces and resources only. The
aiogram software is licensed under the MIT License, and that license is not
affected by this section.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders, that is the project maintainers and the administrators of
the official chats, are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive,
or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, messages, and other contributions
that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for
moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
Examples of representing our community include using an official e-mail
address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an
appointed representative at an online or offline event.

It also applies to conduct outside community spaces when that conduct has a
substantial negative impact on the safety and well-being of community members,
for example harassment of a contributor through private messages that started
in a project chat.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement by email at
[github@aiogram.dev](mailto:github@aiogram.dev). Reports may also be sent to
the admins of the Telegram chat, or through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/aiogram/aiogram/security/advisories/new)
when a report involves a repository. For a complaint about a chat
administrator, use the email address.

When reporting, please include, where possible: where the incident happened
(a link to the issue, pull request, or chat), who was involved, what happened,
the approximate time, and screenshots or message links. You may report on
behalf of someone else.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. We aim to
acknowledge every report within a few days.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the
reporter of any incident. The identity of a reporter will not be shared with
the reported person without the reporter's consent.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining
the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct.
Steps may be skipped for severe or repeated violations.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed
unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the
behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested. In Telegram
chats, the offending message may be deleted.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of
actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No
interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with
those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This
includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels
like social media or private messages. In Telegram chats, this may be a
temporary mute. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including
sustained inappropriate behavior, spam, or harassment.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public
communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or
private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction
with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period.
Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community
standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment of an
individual, aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals,
scams, or ban evasion.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the
community. Bans may be applied across all project spaces, including the GitHub
organization and all official Telegram chats.

### Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, you may appeal by
writing to [github@aiogram.dev](mailto:github@aiogram.dev) within 30 days,
explaining why you believe the decision was wrong. Appeals are reviewed by a
community leader who was not involved in the original decision whenever
possible. One well-reasoned message is enough.

Community leaders who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good
faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by the
project leadership, up to removal of their maintainer or administrator role.
