# 09 — 3.x quick wins (pure Python, ship now)

Independent of v4. Each is a small, focused PR with a `CHANGES/` fragment and tests, **measured against its own
baseline** (D120) with the A-05 harness. Together they become the honest baseline the v4 spike must beat (01 §5).
D120 dropped #6 and #15, reworked #2, and attached compatibility notes to #1, #10, #18 and #21–22. PD-28 then
dropped #18, made #8 measure-or-drop and gave #21 its per-update-on-failure shape. **Behaviour-changing items ship in
a 3.x minor**, not a patch release (PD-28). The plans live in [`../plans/3x-quick-wins/`](../plans/3x-quick-wins/README.md).
