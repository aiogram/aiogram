Fixed :class:`aiogram.types.message.Message` ``reply_*`` shortcuts sending
``callback_query_id`` via the deprecated top-level field while the filled
``ephemeral_message_parameters`` object carried ``null``. Both
``callback_query_id`` and ``replace_callback_query_message`` (which previously
had no route through ``reply_*`` at all) are now routed into the filled
``ephemeral_message_parameters`` object, and the deprecated scalar is no
longer sent.
